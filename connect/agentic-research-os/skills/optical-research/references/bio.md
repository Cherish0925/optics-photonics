# bio

MCX and MMC propagate photon packets through turbid media; they generally model energy/time transport rather than optical phase. Use wave or full-wave methods for coherent interference or subwavelength effects. Choose voxel or mesh geometry according to the tissue model.

Record absorption coefficient, scattering coefficient, anisotropy g, refractive index and coefficient length units. Reduced scattering mu_s'=(1-g)mu_s is not interchangeable with mu_s at fixed phase function. Specify source and detector geometry, packet normalization, boundary reflection and time bins. Compare statistical uncertainty as packet count changes, independently of voxel/mesh resolution. Keep fluence, fluence rate, deposited energy and detected power distinct when connecting to measurements.

Use software_details for the selected tool’s primary documentation and implementation links.
