# xray

SRW supplies synchrotron radiation/wave propagation, SHADOW4 geometric beamline tracing, and xrt radiation and optical-element models. Source coherence, bandwidth, emittance and diffraction determine which representation is useful.

Record photon energy and wavelength conversion, spectral bandwidth convention, electron-beam parameters, element coordinates and polarization. Crystal diffraction requires lattice/reflection, orientation and material response rather than a visible-light mirror approximation. Separate flux per bandwidth, spectral brightness and power. Preserve source covariance and phase/coherence when coupling a ray beamline to wave propagation. Verify apertures and coordinate rotations before interpreting very small beam footprints.
