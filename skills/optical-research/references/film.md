# film

Planar stacks admit transfer/scattering matrix methods. tmm and Solcore provide open Python APIs; Essential Macleod, OptiLayer and TFCalc offer specialized workflows. Decide which layers are coherent, and whether roughness, anisotropy or lateral patterning invalidate a scalar isotropic model.

Supply vacuum wavelength, angle, polarization, complex refractive index and thickness with a common unit. Use the upstream time/sign convention for absorption. Record the material dataset, interpolation rule, temperature and valid spectral interval. For nonabsorbing entrance/exit media verify R+T=1 in a lossless stack. With absorbing entrance media, 1-R-T is not automatically internal absorption normalized to incident power; use the solver's power-entering definition. Fit measured spectra with identifiable parameters and retain uncertainty/correlation rather than reporting only a best thickness.
